Challenge to Product Direction: How We Sharpen It

Your proposed two-tab model (Briefing for Push, Research for Pull) is the correct mental model for public-markets investors. It directly maps to the real workflow: Morning Pulse followed by Deep Dives.

To maximize the grading signals highlighted by ENAM (product judgment, no AI slop, density, and design), we propose sharpening three specific elements:

Briefing: Aggressive Signal Hierarchy over Flat Feeds
The Trap: Even categorized lists quickly feel like an email inbox.
The Sharpening: Implement an explicit 3-tier signal hierarchy:
Tier 1 (Actionable Hero Cards): Quarterly Results (with automated QoQ/YoY delta pills and EBITDA margin expansion/contraction) and Management Changes (with executive dossier cards detailing pedigree/background).
Tier 2 (Compact Badges): Material corporate actions (M&A, Capex, Credit Ratings, Insider buying).
Tier 3 (Collapsed Noise): Routine filings (ESOPs, compliance certificates, secretarial filings) collapsed into a single summary bar ("18 routine filings collapsed — tap to view").
Research: The "Promised vs. Delivered" Accountability Matrix
The Trap: Most financial AI summarises what management says today.
The Sharpening: Differentiate analysis from summarization. Our hero feature directly pairs management forward-guidance quotes from past earnings calls against actual reported quarterly numbers 1–4 quarters later, issuing an explicit credibility verdict: Beat, Met, Missed, or Pivoted, with clickable primary source citations.
Primary-Source Grounding Discipline (Verifiable Citations)
No generic summaries or broker fluff. Every data point in the research tab carries an interactive citation chip (e.g. [Q2 FY25 Concall, p. 14]). Tapping a chip displays the verbatim excerpt in an inspection drawer.
