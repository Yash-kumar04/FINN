Brief Profile (in case of appointment) 
Mr. Prithvi Vachani is currently serving as the Vice President at Dixon 
Technologies (India) Limited. A 2017 graduate of Bentley University, Mr. Prithvi 
quickly demonstrated his strategic acumen upon entering the professional 
arena. His exceptional foresight and leadership were notably displayed when 
he successfully orchestrated a major collaboration with boAt, India’s leading 
audio product company, resulting in the production of innovative twin wireless 
speakers. Mr. Prithvi has been at the forefront of Dixon’s ambitious expansion, 
spearheading the establishment of a state-of-the-art facility in Chennai. 
Through strategic partnerships with industry giants like Asus and HP, he has 
driven the rapid growth of Dixon’s IT hardware division, solidifying its position 
as a key player in the tech manufacturing landscape. 
 
A proponent of continuous learning and global perspectives, Mr. Prithvi 
pursued a Master of Business Administration at the prestigious London 
Business School. Equipped with this advanced degree, he has played a pivotal 
role in Dixon’s mobile division, managing key accounts such as Jio and Xiaomi. 
His strategic insights and hands-on leadership have been instrumental in 
building strong partnerships and significantly contributing to the company’s 
success. Mr. Prithvi is committed to positioning Dixon Technologies as an 
engineering powerhouse. He is leading efforts to diversify into new product 
categories, ensuring that the company remains at the forefront of innovation 
and continues to scale new heights in the rapidly evolving tech industry.
